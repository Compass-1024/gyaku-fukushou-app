Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

This page explains how Oboe-Tore (the "App") handles user information.

1. Basic architecture of this App

Most of this App runs as a backend-free SPA (Single Page Application), and data you enter or record is stored only in your own browser's localStorage by default. No data is sent to any third-party server, including ours.

The one exception: if you turn on "Reminder Notifications" in Settings (an opt-in feature), the minimum information needed to deliver notifications is sent to this App's server. See "3. About reminder notifications" below. This feature is off by default, and no data leaves your device unless you turn it on.

2. Data we store

Data Contents Stored in
Training history Mode, level, correct count, timestamp (up to 200 records) Browser localStorage (gyaku-fukushou:history)
App settings Theme, speech rate/voice, sound effects on/off and volume, daily goal, reminder notification setting Browser localStorage (gyaku-fukushou:settings)
Push subscription info (only if Reminder Notifications is on) Push subscription details (endpoint, etc.), whether you played that day, and your UI language setting (used only to pick the notification's language) This App's server (Redis storage via Vercel Serverless Functions)

We do not collect or store personally identifying information such as your name, email address, or location.

3. About reminder notifications

"Reminder Notifications" in Settings is an opt-in feature that's off by default. When enabled, if you haven't played at all that day, we send a reminder push notification around 9 PM JST each day (the exact time may vary).

  • Turning it on triggers your browser's own notification permission dialog. If you don't allow it, this feature can't be used.
  • On iOS, this only works when the App has been added to the Home Screen (a limitation of the Web Push standard — it doesn't work from a Safari tab).
  • Turning it on sends the minimum information needed for delivery (the push subscription info issued by your browser — i.e. an endpoint — the date you last played, and your UI language setting) to this App's server (Vercel Serverless Functions), stored in Redis via Vercel. The UI language setting is used only to pick the notification message's language (Japanese/English). The contents of your training history (correct counts, question content, etc.) are never sent to the server.
  • Delivery itself goes through your browser vendor's push service as required by the Web Push standard (Chrome/Edge/Android → Google, Firefox → Mozilla, Safari/iOS → Apple). This is part of the Web Push standard, not a third party we chose ourselves.
  • Turning it back off deletes the subscription info stored on the server. Revoking notification permission in your browser also stops delivery.

4. About microphone (voice) use

Word Mode uses your browser's speech recognition feature (the Web Speech API's SpeechRecognition) to check your spoken answers.

  • Using the microphone triggers your browser's own permission dialog. If you don't allow it, Word Mode's speech recognition can't be used.
  • This App itself (its developer/operator) never collects, stores, or sends voice data.
  • That said, the SpeechRecognition API's implementation depends on your browser — in some browsers (e.g. Google Chrome), voice data may be sent to the browser vendor's servers (e.g. Google) for processing. This is a property of the browser itself, not something this App controls. See your browser's own privacy policy for details.
  • Similarly, the read-aloud feature (SpeechSynthesis) relies on your browser/OS's own speech synthesis engine.

5. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This App uses no cookies, and includes no analytics tools or ad-tracking of any kind.

6. Sharing with third parties

We never share or provide your training history or app settings to any third party (since this data never leaves the App in the first place). Only when Reminder Notifications is on does the minimal information described in "3. About reminder notifications" pass through this App's server and the Web Push delivery path (your browser vendor's push service).

7. Deleting your data

To delete your stored data, use "Delete all training history" in Settings. You can also clear it by removing this site's data (localStorage) from your browser settings. If Reminder Notifications was on, turning it off in Settings deletes the subscription info stored on the server.

You can also use "Export backup" in Settings to download all your stored data (training history and app settings) as a JSON file. "Restore from backup" on the same screen lets you load that file on another device or browser (this overwrites whatever is currently stored).

8. Deployment and hosting

This App is hosted on Vercel. Access logs and similar data may be generated as part of Vercel's standard infrastructure operations — this is not something the App itself collects. When Reminder Notifications is used, notification delivery and subscription storage also run on Vercel's infrastructure (Serverless Functions and Redis storage).
